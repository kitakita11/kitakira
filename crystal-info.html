Crystals are gifts from billions of years of geology. Every natural crystal carries unique energy and story, formed deep within the earth over countless millennia.

How Crystals Form

Crystal formation takes millions to billions of years under extreme underground conditions.

  • Magma formation：When magma cools slowly underground, mineral atoms arrange into regular crystal lattices.
  • Hydrothermal veins：Hot mineral-rich water flows through rock cracks, minerals precipitate and grow crystals.
  • Sedimentary formation：Minerals deposit layer by layer in caves or seawater, forming agate and calcite.
  • Metamorphic formation：Rocks under high pressure and temperature recrystallize into garnet, jade etc.

Legends & Stories of Crystals

Since ancient civilizations, humans regarded crystals as sacred stones.

  • Ancient Egypt：Cleopatra wore rose quartz, believed it preserved youth and beauty. Quartz was used in ritual amulets.
  • Ancient Greece：People thought clear quartz was frozen eternal ice from gods, impossible to melt.
  • Chinese tradition：Jade represents virtue, purity and protection. It was the stone of nobles for thousands of years.
  • Medieval Europe：Amethyst was thought to guard against intoxication and evil spirits.

Crystal Origins & Mining Locations

Different geological environments produce crystals with different colours and textures.

  • Brazil — The world’s largest crystal producer: amethyst, citrine, clear quartz, tourmaline.
  • Madagascar — Famous for rare & colourful crystals: rose quartz, morganite, labradorite.
  • South Africa — Tiger’s eye, garnet and many oxide minerals.
  • USA (Arkansas) — High-quality clear quartz clusters.
  • China — Jade, agate, fluorite and abundant quartz resources.
  • India — Moonstone, rutile quartz and carnelian.

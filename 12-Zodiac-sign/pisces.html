Pisces ♓

Feb 19 - Mar 20 | Water Element · Ruled by Neptune & Jupiter

Myth & History

Pisces are two fish bound by a cord, escaping the monster Typhon. They symbolise empathy, dreams, spirituality, imagination and the thin boundary between reality and the spiritual realm.

Character Traits

Compassionate, intuitive, artistic and gentle. Pisces are deeply empathetic, easily absorb surrounding emotions, with vivid imagination and strong spiritual sensitivity.

Background & Energy

Pisces is mutable water energy ruled by Neptune & Jupiter. It governs dreams, intuition, compassion and spiritual connection. This energy enhances creativity, empathy and soft spiritual healing.

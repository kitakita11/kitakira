Libra ♎

Sep 23 - Oct 23 | Air Element · Ruled by Venus

Myth & History

Libra is the scales of justice held by Astraea, goddess of fairness. It is the only non-living symbol in the zodiac. Ancient cultures used balance scales to represent equal judgement, harmony and the search for fairness in human relationships.

Character Traits

Charming, diplomatic, peaceful and lover of beauty. Libra hates conflict and strives for balance in relationships. They appreciate art, aesthetics and treat others politely, but may struggle with hard decisions.

Background & Energy

Libra is cardinal air, representing partnership, beauty, negotiation and harmony. Its energy teaches us to see both sides, build fair relationships and bring grace and beauty into daily life.

Recommended Crystals

  • Rose Quartz - attract harmonious relationships
  • Blue Lace Agate - smooth communication
  • Opal - enhance charm & aesthetic sense
  • Peridot - release relationship tension
  • Clear Quartz - support balanced judgement

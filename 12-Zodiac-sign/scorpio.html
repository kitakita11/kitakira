Scorpio ♏

Oct 24 - Nov 22 | Water Element · Ruled by Pluto & Mars

Myth & History

Scorpio comes from the scorpion that killed Orion. It represents transformation, secrets, deep passion, death and rebirth, digging into hidden truths and the power of inner renewal.

Character Traits

Intense, passionate, perceptive and loyal. Scorpios feel deeply, seek truth and have strong willpower. They embrace transformation and rebirth after hardship.

Background & Energy

Scorpio is fixed water energy, about deep emotional transformation. It supports releasing old wounds, uncovering truth and powerful inner renewal.

Recommended Crystals

  • Obsidian - protection & releasing shadows
  • Malachite - transformation & healing trauma
  • Garnet - deep passion & inner strength
  • Smoky Quartz - ground intense emotions
  • Rhodonite - heal heart wounds

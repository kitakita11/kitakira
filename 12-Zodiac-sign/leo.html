Leo ♌

Jul 23 - Aug 22 | Fire Element · Ruled by Sun

Myth & History

Leo stands for the Nemean Lion from Heracles’ twelve trials. The lion’s hide could not be pierced by any weapon. Heracles defeated it and wore its pelt, and the lion was placed in the sky as a constellation.

Character Traits

Confident, generous, warm-hearted and dramatic. Leo loves to shine, inspire others and enjoy admiration. They are loyal, big-hearted natural leaders with bright energy.

Background & Energy

Leo is fixed fire ruled by the Sun, the centre of our solar system. Leo energy is self-expression, joy, creativity and courage to show your true self. It represents vitality and generosity.

  • Ruby - ignite passion & courage
  • Citrine - attract success & abundance
  • Clear Quartz - amplify bright energy

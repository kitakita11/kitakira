Gemini ♊

May 21 - Jun 21 | Air Element · Ruled by Mercury

Myth & History

Gemini represents twin brothers Castor and Pollux in Greek legend. Pollux was immortal while Castor was mortal. After Castor died, Pollux begged Zeus to share his immortality, so they were placed as twins in the sky forever.

Character Traits

Curious, witty, quick-minded and talkative. Gemini loves learning new things, communication and change. They have flexible thinking and can see multiple sides of one story.

Background & Energy

Gemini is mutable air, symbolizing communication, information exchange and duality. This energy helps gather ideas, connect people and adapt quickly to new environments.

Recommended Crystals

  • Citrine - sharpen thinking & creativity
  • Blue Lace Agate - calm scattered thoughts
  • Amazonite - support honest communication
  • Clear Quartz - clear mental confusion
  • Apophyllite - open mind & curiosity

Aquarius ♒

Jan 20 - Feb 18 | Air Element · Ruled by Uranus (Saturn traditional)

Myth & History

Aquarius is the water bearer, pouring water that nourishes humanity. The symbol represents innovation, humanitarian ideals, independent thought and breaking old outdated rules.

Character Traits

Original, independent, humanitarian and intellectual. Aquarians think outside norms, care about collective good and value freedom of mind. They embrace unique ideas and progress.

Background & Energy

Aquarius is fixed air energy ruled by Uranus. It governs innovation, humanitarian vision and radical change. This energy awakens original thinking and helps you walk your own unique path.

Recommended Crystals

  • Amethyst - spiritual insight & calm
  • Aquamarine - clear vision & truth
  • Iolite - awaken inner vision
  • Labradorite - magic & transformation
  • Clear Quartz - amplify original ideas

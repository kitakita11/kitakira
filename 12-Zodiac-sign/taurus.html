Taurus ♉

Apr 20 - May 20 | Earth Element · Ruled by Venus

Myth & History

Taurus is linked to the white bull in Greek mythology, the disguise of Zeus to abduct Europa. The bull symbolizes strength, fertility and quiet persistence. Ancient civilizations regarded bulls as sacred symbols of harvest and abundance.

Character Traits

Reliable, patient, loyal and sensual. Taurus loves comfort, beauty and stable life. Slow to make decisions but once committed, they stay loyal and persistent to the end.

Background & Energy

Taurus is fixed earth energy, representing steady growth, material security and enjoying earthly beauty. It teaches us to slow down, appreciate the present and build lasting foundations.

Sagittarius ♐

Nov 23 - Dec 21 | Fire Element · Ruled by Jupiter

Myth & History

Sagittarius is the wise centaur Chiron, teacher and healer of Greek heroes. The archer symbolises adventure, philosophy, truth-seeking, freedom and expanding horizons.

Character Traits

Optimistic, adventurous, honest and philosophical. Sagittarians crave freedom, love learning and exploring new lands and ideas. They are generous, open-minded and seekers of truth.

Background & Energy

Sagittarius is mutable fire energy ruled by Jupiter. It governs expansion, higher wisdom, travel and optimistic growth. This energy opens your mind, attracts new opportunities and broadens your world view.

Recommended Crystals

  • Turquoise - protection & adventure
  • Lapis Lazuli - seek truth & wisdom
  • Sodalite - philosophical clarity
  • Amethyst - spiritual expansion
  • Citrine - attract lucky opportunities

Capricorn ♑

Dec 22 - Jan 19 | Earth Element · Ruled by Saturn

Myth & History

Capricorn is the sea-goat, half goat half fish. It climbed from deep waters up to mountain peaks. It represents ambition, discipline, patience, long-term goals and hard-earned success.

Character Traits

Ambitious, disciplined, responsible and practical. Capricorns build slowly and steadily. They value structure, tradition and work tirelessly to reach long-term dreams.

Background & Energy

Capricorn is cardinal earth energy ruled by Saturn. It governs career, responsibility, endurance and solid achievement. This energy supports perseverance and turning big plans into real results.

Recommended Crystals

  • Garnet - ambition & vitality
  • Black Onyx - self-control & protection
  • Smoky Quartz - release burdens
  • Malachite - transform obstacles
  • Tiger’s Eye - steady career confidence

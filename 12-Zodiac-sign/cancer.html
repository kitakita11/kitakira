Cancer ♋

Jun 22 - Jul 22 | Water Element · Ruled by Moon

Myth & History

Cancer is the giant crab sent by Hera to attack Heracles during his twelve labours. The crab bit Heracles’ foot, but was crushed. Hera placed the crab into the night sky as a reward for its loyalty and bravery.

Character Traits

Nurturing, sensitive, protective and deeply emotional. Cancer values family, home and memories. They are caring guardians, easily absorb surrounding emotions and protect loved ones fiercely.

Background & Energy

Cancer is cardinal water, ruled by the ever-changing Moon. It represents home, maternal love, emotional safety and inner feelings. Its energy connects us to family roots and emotional healing.

Recommended Crystals

  • Moonstone - balance emotions & intuition
  • Rose Quartz - heal heart & family bonds
  • Chrysocolla - calm mood swings
  • Blue Calcite - release emotional burden
  • Clear Quartz - protect personal aura

Aries ♈

Mar 21 - Apr 19 | Fire Element · Ruled by Mars

Myth & History

In Greek mythology, Aries represents the golden ram whose fleece became the treasure Jason and the Argonauts set out to retrieve. The ram sacrificed itself to save two royal children, and its image was placed among the stars.

Character Traits

Bold, passionate, courageous and pioneering. As the first sign of the zodiac, Aries loves new challenges, acts quickly and hates hesitation. They are natural leaders with strong energy.

Background & Energy

Aries marks the start of spring, symbolizing new beginnings, awakening and raw life force. Its fire energy drives action, bravery and self-confidence.

  • Sunstone - ignites vitality and confidence
  • Tiger's Eye - strengthens willpower
  • Aquamarine - calms impulsive mood
  • Clear Quartz - amplifies positive energy

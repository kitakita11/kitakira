Virgo ♍

Aug 23 - Sep 22 | Earth Element · Ruled by Mercury

Myth & History

Virgo is linked to the goddess of justice and harvest in Greek mythology, Astraea. She left Earth when humanity became corrupted and ascended to heaven, becoming the virgin constellation. Virgo symbolises purity, service and careful judgement.

Character Traits

Detail-oriented, practical, analytical and helpful. Virgo loves organising, refining things and serving others. They notice small details easily and pursue improvement, sometimes being overly self-critical.

Background & Energy

Virgo is mutable earth energy. It connects with daily routine, healing, practical skills and humble service. This energy helps us organise life, fix problems and care for our physical and mental health.

Recommended Crystals

  • Carnelian - reduce overthinking
  • Amazonite - release self-criticism
  • Smoky Quartz - ground anxious thoughts
  • Clear Quartz - sharpen clear analysis
